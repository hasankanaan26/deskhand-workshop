# Nimbus Electronics — Limited Warranty

Coverage for product defects after the refund and return windows close.

## Coverage

All Nimbus-branded products carry a **1-year limited warranty** from the date of
delivery, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Examples of
covered defects: dead pixels beyond the panel manufacturer's threshold, battery
failure to hold charge, buttons or switches that stop registering, connectivity
hardware faults.

Third-party products sold on nimbuselectronics.com carry the manufacturer's own
warranty; support should hand the customer the manufacturer's claim link rather than
processing the claim in-house.

## Claim Process

1. Verify the delivery date puts the item inside the 1-year window.
2. Ask for a short description of the defect and, where visible, a photo or video.
3. Run the relevant troubleshooting script (power cycle, firmware update, reset).
   Skip this step if the defect is clearly physical.
4. If the defect is confirmed or cannot be ruled out remotely, approve the claim.

Customers are never charged shipping on warranty claims.

## Remedies

The default remedy is a **replacement of the same model**, shipped on claim approval.
A **refund is offered only when a replacement is out of stock** with no restock date
within 14 days. Refunds under warranty follow the refund policy's approval
thresholds.

If the same unit is replaced twice for the same defect, escalate to a team lead —
the third remedy is customer's choice of any remedy including refund.

## Exclusions

Not covered: accidental damage (drops, spills), cosmetic wear, damage from
unauthorized repair or modification, consumables (ear tips, cables under $10), use
outside the published operating specifications, and damage to third-party equipment
alleged to be caused by a Nimbus product — those claims go to the liability desk via
escalation, never through warranty.
