# Nimbus Electronics — Refund Policy

This policy governs monetary refunds issued to customers for purchases made on
nimbuselectronics.com. It supersedes all prior versions of the refund policy.

## Scope

Applies to all products purchased directly from Nimbus Electronics, whether paid by
card, store credit, or gift card. Purchases made through third-party marketplaces are
governed by the marketplace's own refund process and are out of scope.

## Window

Refund requests must be submitted within **14 days of delivery**, as recorded by the
carrier's delivery confirmation. Requests received after this window are not eligible
for a refund and should be redirected to warranty service where applicable.

If no delivery confirmation exists, the window is measured as 14 days from the
estimated delivery date plus 3 calendar days of grace.

## Approval Thresholds

- Refunds of **$50.00 or less** may be approved automatically by the support system.
- Refunds **above $50.00** require approval by a support team lead or manager before
  the refund is issued.
- A refund may never exceed the total paid for the order, including tax and shipping,
  under any circumstances.

## Final Sale

Items marked **Final Sale** or **Clearance — Final Sale** at the time of purchase are
not eligible for refunds. This applies regardless of the reason for the request,
except where the item is defective on arrival, in which case the warranty policy
applies instead.

## Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Where the original method is
unavailable (expired card, closed account), store credit is issued instead. Gift card
purchases are always refunded as store credit.

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are submitted to the payment processor within 1 business day.
Customers should be told to expect the funds within 5–10 business days depending on
their bank. Do not promise a specific date.
