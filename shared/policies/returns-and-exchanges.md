# Nimbus Electronics — Returns & Exchanges

This document describes how customers return or exchange physical products, including
packaging and shipping requirements.

## Returns Window

We want customers to be confident buying from Nimbus. Items may be returned for a
full refund within **30 days of delivery**. The customer initiates the return through
their account page or by contacting support; support generates a prepaid return label.

## Condition Requirements

Returned items must be:

- In their original packaging, with all included accessories and documentation.
- Free of physical damage beyond reasonable inspection wear.
- Deregistered from any Nimbus cloud account (for connected devices).

Items returned incomplete or damaged may be subject to a restocking deduction of up
to 20% of the item price, at the warehouse team's discretion.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size, color, or model are processed as a return plus a new
order. If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference; if less,
the difference is refunded. Exchanges for the identical item (e.g. wrong color
shipped) ship immediately and do not wait for the return to arrive.

## Return Shipping

Return shipping is free when the return reason is our error (wrong item, defective on
arrival, damaged in transit). For all other reasons, a flat $6.95 label fee is
deducted from the refund unless the customer is a Nimbus+ member.

## Exclusions

The following cannot be returned: opened software licenses, gift cards, hygiene-
sensitive items (earbuds with used ear tips are evaluated case by case), and items
missing their serial number label.
