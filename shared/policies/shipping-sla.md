# Nimbus Electronics — Shipping Service Levels

Commitments we make to customers about delivery, and what we owe them when we miss.

## Delivery Windows

| Service | Promise (business days) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–7 |
| Expedited | 2–3 |
| Next Day | 1 (orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time) |

The window starts when the order status changes to `shipped`, not when the order is
placed. Orders in `processing` for more than 2 business days should be flagged to the
fulfillment team.

## Delay Compensation

If a shipment arrives more than **7 business days late** relative to the promised
window, the customer is entitled to a **$10 account credit**, applied automatically
on request. One credit per order. Delays caused by carrier-verified weather events or
an incorrect customer-provided address do not qualify.

Expedited and Next Day shipping fees are refunded in full whenever the promised
window is missed by any margin.

## Tracking

Every shipment gets a tracking number within 24 hours of leaving the warehouse. If a
customer reports a tracking number that has not updated in 5 or more days, treat the
package as potentially lost and open a carrier trace — do not tell the customer to
"keep waiting."

## Lost Packages

A package is considered lost when the carrier trace is inconclusive after 10 business
days, or immediately when the carrier confirms loss. For lost packages, offer a
replacement shipment first; if the customer prefers, a full refund of the order.
Either remedy requires the carrier trace reference on the ticket.
