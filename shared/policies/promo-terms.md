# Nimbus Electronics — Promotional Pricing Terms

How discounts, promo codes, and bundles interact with refunds and returns.

## Eligibility

Promotional prices apply only at the time of purchase. Price adjustments after
purchase (e.g. an item goes on sale the next day) are granted once per customer per
quarter, within 7 days of the order date, as store credit for the difference.

## Refunds On Promo Items

Refunds for items bought at a promotional price are calculated on the **price
actually paid, never the list price**. Supporting rules:

- Single discounted item: refund = paid price for that item.
- **Bundle purchases**: if part of a bundle is returned, the discount is recalculated
  as if the returned item had never been in the cart. The refund is the paid bundle
  price minus the recalculated price of the kept items — which is typically **less
  than the returned item's standalone price**. Support tooling computes this;
  do not estimate it by hand.
- Free promotional gifts must be returned with the qualifying item, or their list
  price is deducted from the refund.

## Code Misuse

Orders placed with promo codes obtained through unauthorized distribution (coupon
scraping sites, expired-code forums) may be cancelled before shipment. After
delivery, honor the price but flag the account for the fraud team. Do not debate
code validity with the customer in writing.

## Stacking

Promo codes do not stack with each other or with Nimbus+ member pricing unless the
promotion's published terms say otherwise. If the checkout system incorrectly allowed
stacking, we honor the charged price — the error is ours.
