The same three schedules, as one workflow

Nimbus has three reminder schedules: retail, business and subscriptions. Here each one is drawn the way a workflow tool holds it: a box per step, and every rule (quiet hours, the weekend, the weekly cap, payments, replies) copied into every stage. On the happy path it works. Count the boxes, then find the copies that drifted apart.

Drawn from a list in cadence-as-steps.js; nothing here calls the engine or a model. The four-box version that really runs is steps.py (py steps.py). The engine keeps one copy of every rule: rules.choose_next_action reads the schedule from policies.json on every wake-up.

    When steps are the right call
    
    
    One schedule, one time zone, nothing arriving half-way: then four boxes in a row are easier to read than an engine, and steps.py is all you need. A workflow tool also gives you durable timers for free. The canvas above is what the same idea costs at three schedules, nine rules and six kinds of reply. The engine keeps the durable wait (a wake-up time in the case row) and moves the schedule into data.
