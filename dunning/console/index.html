    The cadence

    The stages are rows in policies.json, not boxes in the code. "Move to the next reminder" is a lookup into the policy, so a new stage is a new row and a version bump. A stage counts from the invoice due date (D) and becomes eligible at 00:00 UTC that day; when it goes out is the compliance rules' business.

    They pay (the ledger tells us; no model involved)
    They reply (the model reads it; code decides)

    Four doors (a timer, a payment, a reply, a person's decision) and one entry point: engine.evaluate. It always starts by re-reading the case, so a reminder is never sent for a balance that was paid in the meantime. The model never touches the balance: a reply can pause, flag or escalate a case, never pay it.
